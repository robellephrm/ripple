# NVIDIA Corporation (NASDAQ: NVDA) — demo memo

Written as a sample input for the Ripple README. It is not a recommendation and holds no position.
Position for the demo: long.

## Business
NVIDIA designs the GPUs and the networking (NVLink, InfiniBand and Spectrum-X Ethernet) that most
AI data centers are built on, and sells them as full rack-scale systems. TSMC makes the chips and
packages them with CoWoS; SK hynix, Samsung and Micron supply the HBM memory; Foxconn, Quanta and
Wistron assemble the systems. The biggest buyers are the four US hyperscalers (Microsoft, Amazon,
Alphabet, Meta), Oracle and the newer GPU clouds.

## Thesis
There are two things the market is getting right, and the long rests on both holding.

1. **Demand runway.** The largest cloud companies keep raising AI capex into next year, so data
   center demand is set by their budgets, not by a cycle turning. This breaks if two of the four
   largest US cloud companies cut their next-year capex plans.
2. **Supply, not demand, is the limit.** Shipments are capped by TSMC's advanced packaging and HBM
   supply rather than by orders, which keeps pricing firm. This breaks if NVIDIA guides gross
   margin below 70% for two quarters in a row.

## KPIs
Management leads with data center revenue growth and gross margin, and the networking business is
the third number to watch, since it shows how much of each rack NVIDIA captures beyond the GPU.

## Risks
Custom chips at the hyperscalers (TPU, Trainium, MTIA, Maia), export rules on China, and customer
concentration among a handful of very large buyers.
